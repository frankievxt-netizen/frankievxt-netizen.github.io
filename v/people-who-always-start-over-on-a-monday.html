People Who Always Start Over on a Monday

There is a reason your new diet always starts on a Monday.

Transcript

If your new diet always starts on a Monday, there is a reason.

In twenty fourteen, Hengchen Dai, Katherine Milkman and Jason Riis studied gym visits and Google searches.

Searches for the word diet jumped at the start of every week, every month and every new year. Gym visits rose after birthdays too.

They called it the fresh start effect. Dates that feel like a new chapter let you separate yourself from your past mistakes.

The old you skipped workouts. The new you, starting Monday, feels like a different person.

It is also why one bad Wednesday can make the whole week feel ruined.

Here is the trick. You do not need to wait for the calendar. Any moment can be a new chapter if you decide it is.

Tomorrow morning is a fresh start too. So is right now.

The science

Based on the fresh start effect (Dai, Milkman and Riis, 2014).

Dai, H., Milkman, K. L., & Riis, J. (2014). The fresh start effect: Temporal landmarks motivate aspirational behavior. Management Science, 60(10), 2563-2582.
