Do Couples Really Start to Look Alike?

The famous idea that couples grow to look alike over the years did not hold up when scientists checked thousands of faces.

Transcript

You have heard couples start to look alike after years together. Scientists checked.

In nineteen eighty seven, Robert Zajonc compared photos of couples on their wedding day and twenty five years later.

He reported that their faces became more similar over time, maybe from sharing the same expressions.

The idea became famous. But that study only had twelve couples.

In twenty twenty, Pin Pin Tea-makorn and Michal Kosinski used face recognition software on over five hundred couples, across many years of photos.

They found no growing resemblance. Couples looked alike from the start, and stayed about the same.

The real effect is simpler. We tend to choose partners who already look a bit like us.

Love does not change your face. It may just be how you picked it.

The science

Based on facial convergence in couples (Zajonc et al., 1987; Tea-makorn and Kosinski, 2020).

Zajonc, R. B., Adelmann, P. K., Murphy, S. T., & Niedenthal, P. M. (1987). Convergence in the physical appearance of spouses. Motivation and Emotion, 11(4), 335-346. Tea-makorn, P. P., & Kosinski, M. (2020). Spouses' faces are similar but do not become more similar with time. Scientific Reports, 10, 17001.
