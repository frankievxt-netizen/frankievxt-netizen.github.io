People With No Voice in Their Head

You might hear a voice in your head all day, but some people barely hear one at all.

Transcript

Right now, you might be hearing these words in your head. Some people do not.

In twenty twenty four, Johanne Nedergaard and Gary Lupyan studied people with very little inner speech.

They called it anendophasia. These people think, plan and feel normally, just not in words.

In tests, they had a harder time remembering lists of words and judging whether two words rhymed.

Talking out loud helped close the gap, as if speech did the job their inner voice usually does.

Most people never question their inner voice. They assume everyone has the same one.

But minds are far more different than we think, even when we look exactly alike.

So be honest. Do you hear a voice in your head right now?

The science

Based on anendophasia (Nedergaard and Lupyan, 2024).

Nedergaard, J. S. K., & Lupyan, G. (2024). Not everybody has an inner voice: Behavioral consequences of anendophasia. Psychological Science, 35(7), 780-797.
