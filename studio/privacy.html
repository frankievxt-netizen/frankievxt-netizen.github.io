Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Channel Studio (also shown on Google sign-in screens under a channel's project name) is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels listed below to upload and manage those channels' own videos. It is not offered to the public.

  • Doomsday Math
  • Atlas of Time
  • Somewhere to Sleep
  • 10,000 Tries
  • Milo's Brick World
  • Wild Mind
  • Quiet Psychology
  • Run It 100 Years

What Google data the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool receives OAuth access and refresh tokens for the YouTube Data API. It uses them only to: upload the owner's videos and thumbnails, set titles, descriptions, tags, playlists and publish times, read the channel's own video list and statistics, and read and reply to comments on the channel's own videos.

How data is used and stored

Tokens and channel data are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sent to any other server, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Nothing is collected from viewers of the channels or of this website, and this website sets no cookies.

Channel Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless; the owner can also delete them from the tool at any time.
