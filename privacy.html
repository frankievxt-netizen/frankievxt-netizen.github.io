Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Quiet Psychology Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the Quiet Psychology channel to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube and TikTok. It is not offered to the public.

What the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in, the tool receives access tokens from YouTube and TikTok that allow it to upload videos to the owner's own accounts. For TikTok it uses the video.upload permission to send videos to the owner's TikTok drafts, and basic profile information to show which account is connected.

Where data is kept

Tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. No data is sent to any other server, sold, or shared with third parties. Nothing is collected from viewers of the channel or of this website.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions) or in the Google account's third-party access settings.
