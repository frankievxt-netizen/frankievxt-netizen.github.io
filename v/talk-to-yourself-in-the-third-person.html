Talk to Yourself in the Third Person

Using your own name when you talk to yourself helps you stay calmer under stress.

Transcript

When you are stressed, try talking to yourself using your own name.

Not why am I so nervous, but why is Alex so nervous?

In twenty fourteen, Ethan Kross and his colleagues tested this before a stressful speech.

People who used their name or you, instead of I, felt less anxious and gave better speeches.

They also worried less afterward about how it went.

This is called distanced self talk. Using your name puts a little space between you and the feeling, like advising a friend.

It is why giving advice to others is easy, and following it yourself is hard.

Next time your mind is spinning, step outside it. Say your name, then say what you would tell a friend.

The science

Based on distanced self talk (Kross et al., 2014).

Kross, E., Bruehlman-Senecal, E., Park, J., Burson, A., Dougherty, A., Shablack, H., Bremner, R., Moser, J., & Ayduk, O. (2014). Self-talk as a regulatory mechanism: How you do it matters. JPSP, 106(2), 304-324.
