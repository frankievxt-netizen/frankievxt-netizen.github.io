People Chose a Bigger Shock to Get It Over With

Some people chose a stronger electric shock just so they would not have to wait for it.

Transcript

Some people would rather take more pain now than wait for less pain later.

In two thousand six, Gregory Berns and his team at Emory University gave people mild electric shocks after different waits.

Then they offered a choice: a stronger shock right now, or a weaker one later.

Some participants chose the bigger shock, just to avoid the waiting.

Brain scans showed that during the wait, areas involved in feeling pain became active in advance.

This is dread. Waiting for something bad is its own kind of suffering.

It is why you sometimes rip off the bandage, or confess quickly just to end the tension.

Often the waiting hurts more than the thing itself. So do the hard thing first.

The science

Based on the neurobiology of dread (Berns et al., 2006).

Berns, G. S., Chappelow, J., Cekic, M., Zink, C. F., Pagnoni, G., & Martin-Skurski, M. E. (2006). Neurobiological substrates of dread. Science, 312(5774), 754-758.
