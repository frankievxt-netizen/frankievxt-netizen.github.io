Why Trips Make You Happier Than Things

Spending on experiences tends to make people happier than spending on things, and the effect lasts longer.

Transcript

The trip will make you happier than the new sofa, and for longer.

In two thousand three, Leaf Van Boven and Thomas Gilovich asked people about purchases they had made to be happy.

Some described an experience, like a concert or a trip. Others described an object, like clothes or gadgets.

Experiences were rated as making people happier.

One reason is adaptation. A new object becomes background in weeks. A memory keeps getting better when you retell it.

Experiences also connect you to other people, and become part of who you are.

You do not tell stories about your phone case. You tell stories about the night the car broke down in the rain.

Next time you have money to spend on happiness, buy a memory.

The science

Based on experiential versus material purchases (Van Boven and Gilovich, 2003).

Van Boven, L., & Gilovich, T. (2003). To do or to have? That is the question. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 85(6), 1193-1202.
